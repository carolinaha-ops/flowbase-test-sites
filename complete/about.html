Company profile

Brightmoor Advisory Partners was founded in 2012 and has offices in Hamburg and Vienna. We have 85 employees, including 9 partners.

Services

  • Operations strategy and process improvement
  • Capacity and resource planning
  • Transformation programme management

Clients

Our clients are mid-sized manufacturers, logistics providers and industrial suppliers in the DACH region.

How we work

Most engagements are billed hourly by consultant grade. Larger programmes are agreed as fixed-fee projects with milestone payments, and some long-term clients use monthly retainers.

Our consultants record their hours in spreadsheets, and project controlling is done in Excel. Partners review utilization and project profitability every month, and we are looking for a better way to report billable hours per project.
